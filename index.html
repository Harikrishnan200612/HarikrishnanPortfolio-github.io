FireGuard – Smart Fire Detection & Safety System

An IoT-based Smart Fire Detection and Safety System that monitors temperature and gas levels in real time and automatically triggers emergency responses to prevent hazards.

The system continuously reads sensor data from a DHT11 temperature sensor and MQ2 gas/smoke sensor. When values exceed safe thresholds, it activates a buzzer alarm, turns on an LED indicator, starts an automatic exhaust fan, and engages a water pump for initial fire suppression — all reverting automatically once conditions normalize.
