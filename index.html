🏅 Patent Application • India

IoT-Based Smart Tunnel Fire Detection and Smoke Extraction System

Application No. 202641050543 A  |  Filed: 21/04/2026  |  Published: 01/05/2026

This invention relates to an IoT-based Smart Tunnel Fire Detection and Smoke Extraction System designed to enhance safety in enclosed environments through real-time monitoring and automated response. The system utilizes temperature, smoke, and gas sensors to continuously collect environmental data within tunnel structures.

A microcontroller analyzes parameters to detect abnormal conditions indicating potential fire hazards. Upon detection, the system automatically activates safety mechanisms — alarms, exhaust fans for smoke extraction, and water sprinkler systems — while sending alerts to authorities through IoT communication.

IoT • Embedded Systems

FireGuard – Smart Fire Detection & Safety System

An IoT-based Smart Fire Detection and Safety System that monitors temperature and gas levels in real time and automatically triggers emergency responses to prevent hazards.

The system continuously reads sensor data from a DHT11 temperature sensor and MQ2 gas/smoke sensor. When values exceed safe thresholds, it activates a buzzer alarm, turns on an LED indicator, starts an automatic exhaust fan, and engages a water pump for initial fire suppression — all reverting automatically once conditions normalize.

ESP32 Arduino IDE Embedded C DHT11 MQ2 Sensor IoT Relay Module

🎯 Applications: Industrial safety, smart homes, laboratories & fire hazard monitoring zones. Demonstrates how IoT and automation enable early detection, real-time alerts, and automated emergency response.
